A Java service my team owned hadn't been touched in two years because everyone was afraid of it. Fixing the fear was a leadership task, not a coding one.

The code wasn't the problem. The absence of tests, docs, and anyone who felt safe changing it was.

What we did, in order:
- Added characterization tests to capture current behavior
- Paired people on it so knowledge spread
- Made small, safe changes to rebuild confidence

Fear compounds in codebases just like it does in teams. The fix is the same: make it safe to act.
