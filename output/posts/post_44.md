The most expensive line item my team manages is GPU time, and the biggest savings came from a culture change, not a code change.

We made efficiency visible and celebrated. Suddenly people cared about utilization the way they care about test coverage.

What shifted the culture:
- A shared dashboard everyone could see
- Shouting out the person who improved utilization, publicly
- Treating waste as a bug, not a fact of life

You can mandate a metric, but you can't mandate that people care. As a manager, making them care is the actual work.
