I stopped asking my team "is the AI working?" and started asking "how would we know if it stopped?"

The first question gets you a confident yes. The second gets you a real engineering conversation.

It surfaces the things that matter:
- What's our quality metric, and who watches it?
- What's the alert when it degrades?
- How fast could we even notice a regression?

The quality of a team's answers is capped by the quality of the manager's questions. With AI, ask about failure, not success.
