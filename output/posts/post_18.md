A junior engineer cut our LLM costs more than any architecture decision I made that quarter. As their manager, here's what I learned from it.

They didn't optimize the model. They read the bill, line by line, and asked "why are we sending this?"

What it taught me about leading AI teams:
- Cost is a feature. Make it visible to everyone, not just finance.
- The person closest to the code often sees the waste first
- Reward the unglamorous wins, not just the launches

Give engineers visibility into the bill and they'll optimize what you'd never think to ask about.
