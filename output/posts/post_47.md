I treat our LLM cost dashboard the same way I treat our error rate: a number that should trend down and never surprise me.

When cost spikes are invisible until the invoice, you're managing blind. We wired token spend into the same observability everything else lives in.

What that gives my team:
- Cost anomalies caught in hours, not at month-end
- A clear before/after for every optimization
- Engineers who can see the impact of their own changes

You can't manage a number you only see once a month. Bring cost into the daily loop and it stops being scary.
