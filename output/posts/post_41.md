The stakeholder question that used to scare me: "Can you just make the AI a bit more accurate?"

It sounds reasonable. It hides a world of tradeoffs. As a manager, my job is to make those tradeoffs visible instead of nodding along.

How I respond now:
- "More accurate on which cases? Improving one often costs another."
- "Here's the cost and latency that accuracy buys."
- "Here's the eval that tells us if we actually got there."

Turning a vague ask into a measurable tradeoff is how AI projects stay sane. Vague goals produce endless work.
