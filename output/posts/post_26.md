I set one rule for my team this year: every LLM feature ships with a cost-per-request number. It changed how we build.

When cost is invisible, engineers optimize for what they can see, latency and accuracy. The bill becomes someone else's surprise.

Making cost a first-class metric did three things:
- Engineers started trimming prompts without being asked
- We caught a runaway feature before it hit the invoice
- 'Is it worth the tokens?' became a normal design question

You get the behavior you measure. Measure cost, and frugality follows.
