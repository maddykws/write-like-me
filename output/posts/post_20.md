"We need more GPUs" is the most expensive sentence a team can say without checking utilization first.

As an engineering manager, my job when I hear it is to ask one question before signing the bill: how busy are the GPUs we already have?

More often than not:
- Utilization is sitting at 20-30%
- The bottleneck is batching or data loading, not compute
- A week of optimization beats a quarter of procurement

Throwing hardware at a software problem feels fast. It's usually just expensive.
