Ten posts from the finish line, the leadership lesson AI has hammered home hardest: speed without direction is just expensive motion.

AI lets teams move faster than ever. That's exactly why direction matters more than ever, fast in the wrong direction is just faster waste.

Where I spend my energy as a manager now:
- Making sure we're solving a real problem before we accelerate
- Keeping quality and cost visible so speed doesn't hide rot
- Protecting the team's focus from every shiny new release

The tools will keep getting faster. Judgment, taste, and direction are the moat. Invest there.
