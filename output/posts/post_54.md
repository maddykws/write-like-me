The hardest conversation I have as an AI team's manager: telling leadership the impressive demo isn't ready to ship.

The pressure is enormous. The demo wowed everyone. Saying 'not yet' feels like sandbagging. But shipping a fragile AI feature erodes trust faster than a delay ever could.

How I hold the line without being the blocker:
- I show the failure cases, not just my opinion
- I name exactly what 'ready' requires and how long
- I offer a smaller, real thing we can ship now

Protecting the user from a half-baked AI feature is protecting the product. That's the job, even when it's unpopular.
