The order of your prompt changes the answer more than most people realize.

Same instructions, same model. Move the instruction from the bottom to the top and quality jumps.

What consistently works:
- Put the task and constraints first
- Give the model a role and a clear output format
- Place long context before the question, not after
- Show one example of exactly what 'good' looks like

Prompt engineering isn't tricks. It's removing ambiguity until there's only one reasonable interpretation.
