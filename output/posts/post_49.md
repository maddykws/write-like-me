Half my team writes Java, half writes Python, and the AI services sit in between. Bridging that divide is one of my quieter responsibilities.

The temptation is to let each camp optimize locally and lob artifacts over the wall. That's how integration bugs are born.

What keeps it healthy:
- Shared contracts (clear API schemas) so neither side guesses
- Cross-pollination: Java folks review Python PRs and vice versa
- One definition of 'done' across the whole pipeline

Polyglot teams don't fail on syntax. They fail on the seams between languages. Manage the seams.
