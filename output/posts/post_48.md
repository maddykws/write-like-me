The phrase that quietly kills AI projects: "we'll figure out evaluation later."

Later never comes. The team ships, moves on, and the feature becomes unmaintainable because no one can safely change it.

So I make evaluation a starting condition, not a finishing one:
- The eval set is part of the design doc, not an afterthought
- 'How will we measure this?' is a question in kickoff
- No eval, no go, the same way we treat no tests

Evaluation isn't the boring part you do at the end. It's the foundation that makes everything else iterable.
