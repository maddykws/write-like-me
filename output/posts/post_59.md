The biggest shift in my engineering management over the last few years: I optimize less for output and more for learning velocity.

AI changes so fast that what my team knows today is half-obsolete in a year. A team that learns quickly will out-execute a team that's merely productive.

How I build for it:
- We run small experiments constantly and share the results
- We treat 'this didn't work, here's why' as valuable output
- We make time to absorb what's new instead of just shipping

Productivity is this quarter's results. Learning velocity is every quarter's results. In a fast field, bet on the second.
