I asked my team to add type hints to our Python services and got pushback. Six months later, no one wants to go back.

The objection was familiar: "Python is dynamic, types are overhead, they slow us down."

What actually happened once we adopted them:
- Onboarding got faster, new folks could read intent from signatures
- Refactors stopped being terrifying
- Code review focused on logic, not 'what does this return?'

As a manager, I've learned the best standards feel like overhead for a week and relief forever after.
